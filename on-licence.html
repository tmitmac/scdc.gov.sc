Alcohol licensing

On-licence

An on-licence allows alcohol to be sold or supplied for consumption on the licensed premises.

What is an on-licence?

You need an on-licence if you intend to sell or supply alcohol to people for consumption at the premises where it is supplied. This includes businesses such as restaurants, bars, taverns, hotels, cafés and other hospitality businesses.

The licence sets out the areas of the premises where alcohol may be supplied, the hours during which alcohol may be sold or supplied, and any additional conditions that apply to the premises.

Who needs an on-licence?

Restaurants & cafés

Restaurants and cafés that sell or supply alcohol for customers to consume with their meals or at the premises.

Bars & taverns

Bars, taverns and similar premises where alcohol is supplied for consumption on site.

Hotels

Hotels and accommodation providers that supply alcohol to guests or customers for consumption on the premises.

BYO restaurants

Restaurants that allow customers to bring their own alcohol onto the premises may also require an on-licence.

Before you apply

Before applying, make sure your proposed premises can lawfully operate as a licensed premises and that you have the information and supporting documents required for your application.

Planning and building requirements

Check that the proposed use of the premises complies with Council planning and building requirements.

Certified manager

Your premises must have an appropriately certified manager available when alcohol is being sold or supplied, as required by the licence conditions.

Host responsibility

You must have appropriate procedures for preventing intoxication, preventing sales to minors and supporting the responsible consumption of alcohol.

Supporting documents

You will need to provide information such as floor plans, proposed trading hours, manager details and other documents relevant to your application.

Trading hours

Your licence will specify your permitted hours

The hours during which alcohol may be sold or supplied will be specified on your licence. When applying, you should provide the normal hours of operation for the premises and explain any proposed late-night trading.

Council may impose conditions on trading hours depending on the circumstances of the premises and the application.

Licence conditions

An on-licence may include conditions relating to how alcohol is supplied and how the premises is operated. Conditions can include:

  • the permitted days and hours for selling alcohol;
  • the areas of the premises where alcohol may be supplied;
  • food and non-alcoholic drink requirements;
  • requirements relating to certified managers;
  • requirements for preventing intoxication and harm;
  • requirements relating to minors and restricted areas.

Applying for an on-licence

1

Prepare your application

Gather your premises information, floor plan, proposed trading hours, manager details and supporting documents.

2

Submit and pay the fee

Submit the completed application to Council and pay the applicable application fee.

3

Application assessment

Council officers and relevant agencies assess the application and may request further information.

4

Decision

The application is considered by the appropriate licensing authority and a decision is issued.

You must have a licence before selling alcohol

Do not begin selling or supplying alcohol until the required licence has been issued and is in force. Operating without the required licence may result in enforcement action.
