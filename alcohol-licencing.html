Overview

Selling or supplying alcohol in Square City

If you intend to sell or supply alcohol as part of a business, organisation or event, you may need an alcohol licence issued by the Square City District Council.

Different types of licences apply depending on the nature of your business, the premises involved and when alcohol will be supplied. Applications are considered under the district's alcohol licensing requirements.

Before applying, make sure you understand the requirements that apply to your proposed premises or event.

Important

You should allow sufficient time for an application to be processed before you intend to begin selling or supplying alcohol.

Licence types

Which licence do you need?

Premises

On-licence

For premises where alcohol is sold or supplied for consumption on the premises, such as restaurants, cafés, bars and entertainment venues.

Learn about on-licences
Premises

Off-licence

For businesses selling alcohol for consumption away from the premises, including bottle shops and certain supermarkets or retailers.

Learn about off-licences
Club

Club licence

For eligible clubs and organisations that supply alcohol to members and authorised guests.

Learn about club licences
Events

Special licence

For temporary events or occasions where alcohol will be supplied outside the usual terms of a premises licence.

Learn about special licences

Applications

Applying for an alcohol licence

The application process generally involves the following steps. Make sure you have the required information and supporting documents before beginning your application.

1

Determine your licence type

Decide whether you require an on-licence, off-licence, club licence or special licence.

2

Prepare your application

Gather details about the applicant, premises or event, proposed trading hours, management arrangements and supporting documentation.

3

Submit your application

Submit the completed application and pay the applicable application fee.

4

Application assessment

The Council and relevant licensing authorities consider the application and any matters raised during the process.

5

Decision

You will be notified of the decision and any conditions that apply to the licence.

Start an application

Before you apply

  • Confirm which type of licence you require.
  • Check that your proposed premises can be used for the intended purpose.
  • Prepare the required supporting documents.
  • Make sure your proposed trading hours are clearly identified.
  • Allow enough time for the application process.

Common documents

  • Completed application form
  • Premises plan or site plan
  • Evidence of the right to occupy the premises
  • Details of managers or responsible persons
  • Additional information required for the application
